Components

Build one React Email component for each, with these names:

EmailLayout · Header · SentBy · Heading · Text · Button · CopyLink · TextLink · StatusLine · Callout · QuotedNote · ChangeList · DetailTable · CompetencyTable · Evidence · Footer · DevBand

Do

  • Say who it is from, what happened and what to do next in the first two lines.
  • Highlight only the evidence cited: 412 of 460 commits.
  • Show status as an icon and a word. Keep subjects to the job and the event.

Do not

  • Put a name, score or CV detail in the subject or preheader.
  • Use the highlighter as decoration, colour alone for status, a second button, an exclamation mark or an emoji.
  • Frame thin evidence as a rejection. It is a gap to close.
