What each role can do

Workspace adminEverything, including members, branding and billing.
RecruiterEvery job: create jobs, draft rubrics, import, request evidence. Cannot approve a rubric or record a decision.
Hiring ownerOnly the jobs they are given: approve rubrics, calibrate, decide.
InterviewerOnly the candidates they interview: submit scorecards. No rankings or other scores.
