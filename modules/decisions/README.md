# @credalent/decisions

Decision packs (AIV-P2-009). A pack belongs to one job and that job's approved rubric version (`@credalent/jobs`), holds a selection of candidates with a snapshot of each one's assessment (`@credalent/assessment`) taken when they joined the pack, and records the hiring owner's decision per candidate. A person makes every decision: recording one stores it, audits it and writes one event, and contacts no candidate (AGENTS.md 1.5). The pack summary moves no stage; recorded decisions do, through AIV-P2-012. Other code imports only `@credalent/decisions` (the `DecisionsContract` types); apps build it with `createDecisions(workspaces, jobs, assessment, audit, now)` from `@credalent/decisions/composition`.

- **Tables:** `decisions.pack` (job and rubric version ids, name, who prepared it and when, status), `decisions.pack_member` (candidate id, the assessment result id, its backed score and "up to N", position), `decisions.decision` (decision, who, when, the reason), `decisions.member_summary` (AIV-P2-011: a member's card text, below), `decisions.outbox` and `decisions.inbox` (the summary job's handled messages). Other modules' ids are opaque: no foreign keys across modules. Migrations are tracked in `migrations.decisions`.
- **Statuses:** `ready` (created with its members) and `decided`. `draft` is in the domain and the check constraint but nothing writes it yet: a pack is created ready. Creating a pack from the ranking (AIV-P2-010) saves no drafts: a pack is created ready, and the ranking adds to an undecided (`ready`) pack.
- **Snapshot:** a member carries the id and the two scores of the candidate's latest assessment on the pack's rubric version, read once. Assessment results are history (a changed score is a new row), so a later score change does not reach a pack. The database backs this for the app role only: it has no `UPDATE` or `DELETE` on `decisions.pack_member`, and on `decisions.pack` it may update the `status` column alone, so a pack cannot move to another job or rubric version. The migrator role, which owns the tables, is not bound by these grants. A candidate must be in the job's ranking on the approved version (`candidate_not_assessed` otherwise); once the job's approved version moves on, nobody new can join the pack.
- **A candidate appears once:** the primary key is `(workspace_id, pack_id, candidate_id)`; `addMembers` ignores a candidate already in the pack, who keeps the snapshot they have. Positions are unique per pack.
- **Who decides:** the member named on the job as its hiring owner, while they hold the `hiring_owner` or `admin` role (`mayDecide`), after `decision.record` checks the role and the job grant. An admin may be named so that a solo recruiter can decide (owner decision (b) in AIV-P1-165, the same as `approvesAs` for the rubric). A recruiter, an admin who is not named, or a hiring owner granted the job but not named on it gets `ForbiddenError` (HTTP 403) and nothing is stored. There is no delegated authority: the workspace model records none (a `shortcut:` in `domain/pack.ts`).
- **Recording:** `recordDecisions` takes one decision (`advance`, `hold`, `do_not_advance`) with a reason for every member, once. The reason is required because the audit log requires one for `decision.recorded` (AGENTS.md 1.5: who, when and why). The pack becomes `decided`; a second record and a late member are refused (`pack_decided`). The app role has no `UPDATE` or `DELETE` on `decisions.decision`, and a decision can only name a member of the pack (foreign key).
- **Card text (AIV-P2-011):** each member's strengths, outstanding risks, Round 1 finding and Credalent's recommendation, written once per member by the worker job `decisions.pack.summarise` (`createPackSummaryHandlers`, on the `assessment` queue it shares, a `shortcut:` in `composition.ts`) after `PackReady.v1` or `PackMembersAdded.v1`, never inside a page request. It reads the snapshot the member joined with (the assessment composition's `readAssessmentAsSystem`), the job's approved rubric names, the candidate's evidence for the job and their identifiers, and asks `@credalent/ai`'s `decisions.summariseMember` once (`domain/summary.ts` writes the facts the model reads, heaviest competency first, and the share of the weight the work meets the need on). Every statement cites the facts or an evidence item by id; a strength cites inspected work, the Round 1 finding the interview notes; a figure is only one a cited quote holds; a CV quote is stored as a claim. The recommendation is decided in code, not by the model (`recommendedAction`): `advance` when the snapshot meets the job's need on at least 50% of the weight, otherwise `hold`, never a rejection, and it is advice only: the pack summary moves no stage; recorded decisions do, AIV-P2-012 (AGENTS.md 1.5). An answer the citation contract or the schema rejects (a source outside the snapshot), no model, a snapshot on a rubric version no longer approved or no readable person stores the member `unavailable`: the page then shows "Summary unavailable" and the snapshot's own strengths. A spent daily AI budget schedules the pack again for 00:05 UTC, and a model that stays unavailable (timeouts, an open circuit) 15 minutes later, storing nothing meanwhile. A member already summarised is skipped, so a redelivery or a later add calls no model again (`decisions.inbox` also skips a redelivered message). The app role may not change or delete a summary; nothing regenerates one yet (shortcut below). `getPack` returns the summaries written so far; a member with none reads "Preparing summary".
- **Contract:** `createPack(ctx, { jobId, name, members })`, `addMembers(ctx, packId, members)`, `listPacks(ctx, jobId)` (AIV-P2-010: the job's packs, newest first, each with its name, status and members' candidate ids, for the ranking's "Add to decision pack" and "In shortlist pack" and the job's Decision tab), `getPack(ctx, packId)` (with the members' summaries), `recordDecisions(ctx, { packId, decisions })`; inputs are validated with zod. Admins and recruiters on the job prepare packs (`pack.prepare`); admins, recruiters and hiring owners on the job read them (`pack.view`); interviewers do not. A hiring owner reaches only the packs of jobs they are granted: a pack on any other job reads as absent.
- **Audit:** each decision is one `decision.recorded` entry about the candidate, with the hiring owner as actor, the reason as note and `{ packId, jobId, decision }`; the entries are the last statements of the transaction (AGENTS.md 3.6). Creating a pack writes one `pack.created` entry about the pack (`{ jobId, rubricVersionId }`), and every member who joins, at creation or later, one `pack.member_added` entry about the candidate (`{ packId, jobId, assessmentId, position }`), with the user who added them as actor and the required reason as the note (AIV-P1-165: "Add to decision pack" asks why), after the pack's other writes (AIV-P1-139). Each member also stores `added_by` and `added_at` (both set or both null: members added before AIV-P1-139 have neither), which the pack page shows as "Added by … on …".
- **Events:** `PackReady.v1` when a pack is created, `PackMembersAdded.v1` when `addMembers` adds anyone (AIV-P2-011), and one `DecisionsRecorded.v1` per `recordDecisions` (`{ packId, jobId }`, strict, opaque ids only), written to `decisions.outbox` in the same transaction. The worker relays this outbox: the summary job consumes the first two, and intake's stage job `DecisionsRecorded.v1` (AIV-P2-012, on the same queue). The events carry no decision: a system consumer reads them with the composition's `readDecisionsAsSystem(tx, workspaceId, packId)`, which is not authorised itself.
- **Decision emails (design update, 22 Nov 2026):** this module owns no "decision email prepared" state. The `DecisionsRecorded.v1` consumer in `@credalent/notifications`, the only place that sends email, creates one draft per person and keeps their state; nothing is sent until a person sends it.
- **Row-level security (ADR-005):** every table `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with `tenantPolicy` on `workspace_id`; the repository also filters by workspace.
- **Limits:** nothing regenerates a member's summary: an `unavailable` one (an answer rejected twice) stays until the "Regenerate" action the ticket mentions is built (a `shortcut:` to lift when the recruiter asks for regeneration). The model is called inside the consumer's transaction, so a large pack on a slow model can outlast the message lock (a `shortcut:` in `composition.ts`). Nothing deletes a pack's rows, so they outlive a candidate's erasure until AIV-P3-014 adds that path, and the demo seed's reset does not purge `decisions.*` (both recorded as `shortcut:`).
- **From the ranking (AIV-P2-010):** apps/web's "Add to decision pack" maps the selected applications to this job's applicants and calls `createPack` or `addMembers` after a preview; `/app/jobs/[jobId]/packs/[packId]` shows a pack's name and members with their snapshots, and the job's Decision tab lists its undecided packs.
- **Recording screen (AIV-P2-012):** `getPack` says whether the reader may record (`mayDecide`); `/app/jobs/[jobId]/packs/[packId]` shows each member's choice ("Advance to Round 2", "Hold", "Do not advance", none chosen at first) to the hiring owner, read-only with "Only the hiring owner can record decisions" to anyone else; on a job with no hiring owner, an admin is offered "Record decisions yourself", which names them the job's hiring owner after a preview with a reason (audited by jobs as `job.hiring_owner_changed`, no email) and then lets them decide (AIV-P1-165) and with who recorded them and when once decided. "Record decisions" needs a choice for everyone, then a preview with one reason, which becomes every decision's note. Intake's consumer moves `advance` to `round2_shortlist` and `do_not_advance` to `not_advanced`; a hold keeps the stage. No candidate is contacted: the decision email drafts are AIV-P2-073.
- **Not here:** client sharing (AIV-P2-013), and the demo seed's "Round 2 shortlist" pack: the seed assesses nobody (see the `shortcut:` in `tools/seed/src/seed.ts`), and a pack needs each member's assessment.
- **Tests:** rules in `src/domain/pack.test.ts` and `src/domain/summary.test.ts`; use cases in `src/application/packs.test.ts` and `src/application/summaries.test.ts`; database scenarios in `tests/integration/src/decisions.test.ts`; the prompt's golden cases in `packages/ai/evals/decisions.summariseMember/`.
