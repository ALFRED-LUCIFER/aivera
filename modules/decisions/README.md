# @credalent/decisions

Decision packs (AIV-P2-009). A pack belongs to one job and that job's approved rubric version (`@credalent/jobs`), holds a selection of candidates with a snapshot of each one's assessment (`@credalent/assessment`) taken when they joined the pack, and records the hiring owner's decision per candidate. A person makes every decision: recording one stores it, audits it and writes one event; it moves no stage and contacts no candidate (AGENTS.md 1.5). Other code imports only `@credalent/decisions` (the `DecisionsContract` types); apps build it with `createDecisions(workspaces, jobs, assessment, audit, now)` from `@credalent/decisions/composition`.

- **Tables:** `decisions.pack` (job and rubric version ids, name, who prepared it and when, status), `decisions.pack_member` (candidate id, the assessment result id, its backed score and "up to N", Credalent's recommendation, position), `decisions.decision` (decision, who, when, the reason) and `decisions.outbox`. Other modules' ids are opaque: no foreign keys across modules. Migrations are tracked in `migrations.decisions`.
- **Statuses:** `ready` (created with its members) and `decided`. `draft` is in the domain and the check constraint but nothing writes it yet: a pack is created ready. Creating a pack from the ranking (AIV-P2-010) decides whether drafts are saved.
- **Snapshot:** a member carries the id and the two scores of the candidate's latest assessment on the pack's rubric version, read once. Assessment results are history (a changed score is a new row), and the app role has no `UPDATE` on `decisions.pack_member`, so a later score change cannot reach a pack. A candidate must be in the job's ranking on the approved version (`candidate_not_assessed` otherwise); once the job's approved version moves on, nobody new can join the pack.
- **A candidate appears once:** the primary key is `(workspace_id, pack_id, candidate_id)`; `addMembers` ignores a candidate already in the pack, who keeps the snapshot they have. Positions are unique per pack.
- **Who decides:** the hiring owner named on the job, while they hold the `hiring_owner` role (`mayDecide`), after `decision.record` checks the role and the job grant. An admin, a recruiter, or a hiring owner granted the job but not named on it gets `ForbiddenError` (HTTP 403) and nothing is stored. There is no delegated authority: the workspace model records none (a `shortcut:` in `domain/pack.ts`).
- **Recording:** `recordDecisions` takes one decision (`advance`, `hold`, `do_not_advance`) with a reason for every member, once. The reason is required because the audit log requires one for `decision.recorded` (AGENTS.md 1.5: who, when and why). The pack becomes `decided`; a second record and a late member are refused (`pack_decided`). The app role has no `UPDATE` or `DELETE` on `decisions.decision`, and a decision can only name a member of the pack (foreign key).
- **Contract:** `createPack(ctx, { jobId, name, members })`, `addMembers(ctx, packId, members)`, `getPack(ctx, packId)`, `recordDecisions(ctx, { packId, decisions })`; inputs are validated with zod. Admins and recruiters on the job prepare packs (`pack.prepare`); admins, recruiters and hiring owners on the job read them (`pack.view`); interviewers do not. A hiring owner reaches only the packs of jobs they are granted: a pack on any other job reads as absent.
- **Audit:** each decision is one `decision.recorded` entry about the candidate, with the hiring owner as actor, the reason as note and `{ packId, jobId, decision }`; the entries are the last statements of the transaction (AGENTS.md 3.6).
- **Events:** `PackReady.v1` when a pack is created and one `DecisionsRecorded.v1` per `recordDecisions` (`{ packId, jobId }`, strict, opaque ids only), written to `decisions.outbox` in the same transaction. Nothing consumes either yet, so the worker does not relay this outbox; they wait unsent. The stage change belongs to a consumer in `@credalent/intake`, which has no contract to move a stage yet.
- **Decision emails (design update, 22 Nov 2026):** this module owns no "decision email prepared" state. The `DecisionsRecorded.v1` consumer in `@credalent/notifications`, the only place that sends email, creates one draft per person and keeps their state; nothing is sent until a person sends it.
- **Row-level security (ADR-005):** every table `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with `tenantPolicy` on `workspace_id`; the repository also filters by workspace.
- **Not here:** creating a pack from the ranking (AIV-P2-010), the pack page (AIV-P2-011), the recording screen (AIV-P2-012), client sharing (AIV-P2-013), finding a job's packs (no list read yet), and the demo seed's "Round 2 shortlist" pack: the seed assesses nobody (see the `shortcut:` in `tools/seed/src/seed.ts`), and a pack needs each member's assessment.
- **Tests:** rules in `src/domain/pack.test.ts`; use cases in `src/application/packs.test.ts`; database scenarios in `tests/integration/src/decisions.test.ts`.
