# @credalent/decisions

Decision packs (AIV-P2-009). A pack belongs to one job and that job's approved rubric version (`@credalent/jobs`), holds a selection of candidates with a snapshot of each one's assessment (`@credalent/assessment`) taken when they joined the pack, and records the hiring owner's decision per candidate. A person makes every decision: recording one stores it, audits it and writes one event, and contacts no candidate (AGENTS.md 1.5). The pack summary moves no stage; recorded decisions do, through AIV-P2-012. Other code imports only `@credalent/decisions` (the `DecisionsContract` types); apps build it with `createDecisions(workspaces, jobs, assessment, audit, now)` from `@credalent/decisions/composition`.

- **Tables:** `decisions.pack` (job and rubric version ids, name, who prepared it and when, status), `decisions.pack_member` (candidate id, the assessment result id, its backed score and "up to N", position), `decisions.decision` (decision, who, when, the reason), `decisions.member_summary` (AIV-P2-011: a member's card text, below), `decisions.outbox` and `decisions.inbox` (the summary job's handled messages). Other modules' ids are opaque: no foreign keys across modules. Migrations are tracked in `migrations.decisions`.
- **Statuses:** `ready` (created with its members) and `decided`. `draft` is in the domain and the check constraint but nothing writes it yet: a pack is created ready. Creating a pack from the ranking (AIV-P2-010) saves no drafts: a pack is created ready, and the ranking adds to an undecided (`ready`) pack.
- **Snapshot:** a member carries the id and the two scores of the candidate's latest assessment on the pack's rubric version, read once. Assessment results are history (a changed score is a new row), so a later score change does not reach a pack. The database backs this for the app role only: it has no `UPDATE` or `DELETE` on `decisions.pack_member`, and on `decisions.pack` it may update the `status` column alone, so a pack cannot move to another job or rubric version. The migrator role, which owns the tables, is not bound by these grants. A candidate must be in the job's ranking on the approved version (`candidate_not_assessed` otherwise); once the job's approved version moves on, nobody new can join the pack.
- **A candidate appears once:** the primary key is `(workspace_id, pack_id, candidate_id)`; `addMembers` ignores a candidate already in the pack, who keeps the snapshot they have. Positions are unique per pack.
- **Who decides:** the hiring owner named on the job, while they hold the `hiring_owner` role (`mayDecide`), after `decision.record` checks the role and the job grant. An admin, a recruiter, or a hiring owner granted the job but not named on it gets `ForbiddenError` (HTTP 403) and nothing is stored. There is no delegated authority: the workspace model records none (a `shortcut:` in `domain/pack.ts`).
- **Recording:** `recordDecisions` takes one decision (`advance`, `hold`, `do_not_advance`) with a reason for every member, once. The reason is required because the audit log requires one for `decision.recorded` (AGENTS.md 1.5: who, when and why). The pack becomes `decided`; a second record and a late member are refused (`pack_decided`). The app role has no `UPDATE` or `DELETE` on `decisions.decision`, and a decision can only name a member of the pack (foreign key).
- **Card text (AIV-P2-011):** each member's strengths, outstanding risks, Round 1 finding and Credalent's recommendation, written once per member by the worker job `decisions.pack.summarise` (`createPackSummaryHandlers`, on the `assessment` queue it shares, a `shortcut:` in `composition.ts`) after `PackReady.v1` or `PackMembersAdded.v1`, never inside a page request. It reads the snapshot the member joined with (the assessment composition's `readAssessmentAsSystem`), the job's approved rubric names, the candidate's evidence for the job and their identifiers, and asks `@credalent/ai`'s `decisions.summariseMember` once (`domain/summary.ts` writes the facts the model reads, heaviest competency first, and the share of the weight the work meets the need on). Every statement cites the facts or an evidence item by id; a strength cites inspected work, the Round 1 finding the interview notes; a figure is only one a cited quote holds; the recommendation is `advance` or `hold`, never a rejection, and is advice only: the pack summary moves no stage; recorded decisions do, AIV-P2-012 (AGENTS.md 1.5). An answer the citation contract or the schema rejects (a source outside the snapshot), a model that stays unavailable, no model, a snapshot on a rubric version no longer approved or no readable person stores the member `unavailable`: the page then shows "Summary unavailable" and the assessment's own strengths. A spent daily AI budget schedules the pack again for 00:05 UTC. A member already summarised is skipped, so a redelivery or a later add calls no model again (`decisions.inbox` also skips a redelivered message). The app role may not change or delete a summary; nothing regenerates one yet (shortcut below). `getPack` returns the summaries written so far; a member with none reads "Preparing summary".
- **Contract:** `createPack(ctx, { jobId, name, members })`, `addMembers(ctx, packId, members)`, `listPacks(ctx, jobId)` (AIV-P2-010: the job's packs, newest first, each with its name, status and members' candidate ids, for the ranking's "Add to decision pack" and "In shortlist pack" and the job's Decision tab), `getPack(ctx, packId)` (with the members' summaries), `recordDecisions(ctx, { packId, decisions })`; inputs are validated with zod. Admins and recruiters on the job prepare packs (`pack.prepare`); admins, recruiters and hiring owners on the job read them (`pack.view`); interviewers do not. A hiring owner reaches only the packs of jobs they are granted: a pack on any other job reads as absent.
- **Audit:** each decision is one `decision.recorded` entry about the candidate, with the hiring owner as actor, the reason as note and `{ packId, jobId, decision }`; the entries are the last statements of the transaction (AGENTS.md 3.6). Creating a pack writes one `pack.created` entry about the pack (`{ jobId, rubricVersionId }`), and every member who joins, at creation or later, one `pack.member_added` entry about the candidate (`{ packId, jobId, assessmentId, position }`), with the user who added them as actor, after the pack's other writes (AIV-P1-139). Each member also stores `added_by` and `added_at` (both set or both null: members added before AIV-P1-139 have neither), which the pack page shows as "Added by … on …".
- **Events:** `PackReady.v1` when a pack is created, `PackMembersAdded.v1` when `addMembers` adds anyone (AIV-P2-011), and one `DecisionsRecorded.v1` per `recordDecisions` (`{ packId, jobId }`, strict, opaque ids only), written to `decisions.outbox` in the same transaction. The worker relays this outbox for the first two, which the summary job consumes; `DecisionsRecorded.v1` has no queue yet and waits unsent. The events carry no decision, and the read a system consumer needs is not built (`getPack` is for members with `pack.view`): it comes with the consumer. The stage change belongs to a consumer in `@credalent/intake`, which has no contract to move a stage yet.
- **Decision emails (design update, 22 Nov 2026):** this module owns no "decision email prepared" state. The `DecisionsRecorded.v1` consumer in `@credalent/notifications`, the only place that sends email, creates one draft per person and keeps their state; nothing is sent until a person sends it.
- **Row-level security (ADR-005):** every table `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with `tenantPolicy` on `workspace_id`; the repository also filters by workspace.
- **Limits:** nothing regenerates a member's summary: an `unavailable` one stays until the "Regenerate" action the ticket mentions is built, and a model outage while a pack is prepared leaves its cards on the assessment's own strengths (a `shortcut:` to lift when the recruiter asks for regeneration). Nothing deletes a pack's rows, so they outlive a candidate's erasure until AIV-P3-014 adds that path, and the demo seed's reset does not purge `decisions.*` (both recorded as `shortcut:`).
- **From the ranking (AIV-P2-010):** apps/web's "Add to decision pack" maps the selected applications to this job's applicants and calls `createPack` or `addMembers` after a preview; `/app/jobs/[jobId]/packs/[packId]` shows a pack's name and members with their snapshots, and the job's Decision tab lists its undecided packs.
- **Not here:** the recording screen (AIV-P2-012), client sharing (AIV-P2-013), and the demo seed's "Round 2 shortlist" pack: the seed assesses nobody (see the `shortcut:` in `tools/seed/src/seed.ts`), and a pack needs each member's assessment.
- **Tests:** rules in `src/domain/pack.test.ts` and `src/domain/summary.test.ts`; use cases in `src/application/packs.test.ts` and `src/application/summaries.test.ts`; database scenarios in `tests/integration/src/decisions.test.ts`; the prompt's golden cases in `packages/ai/evals/decisions.summariseMember/`.
