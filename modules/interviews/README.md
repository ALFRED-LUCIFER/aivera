# @credalent/interviews

Interviews exist to test specific evidence (AIV-P2-001). Each one links a candidate's application on one job (`@credalent/intake`) to a round, an interviewer and the competencies of the job's approved rubric version (`@credalent/jobs`) it must investigate. Other code imports only `@credalent/interviews` (the `InterviewsContract` types); apps build it with `createInterviews(workspaces, jobs, approvedRubric, findApplication, now)` from `@credalent/interviews/composition`, where `approvedRubric` is the jobs composition's `createApprovedRubricReads().approvedRubric` and `findApplication` is the intake composition's. Both are unauthorised reads, called only for the job the use case then authorises; they are passed as functions so intake can call this contract (AIV-P1-114) without a package cycle.

- **Tables:** `interviews.interview` (application, job and candidate ids, round, interviewer user id, `scheduled_at`, status, the rubric version and its `competency_ids`, who created it and when) `interviews.scorecard_drafts` (below), `interviews.meeting_summaries`, `interviews.summary_proposals` and `interviews.inbox` (meeting summaries, below) and `interviews.outbox`. Other modules' ids are opaque: no foreign keys across modules. Migrations are tracked in `migrations.interviews`.
- **Rounds:** `structured_screen` "Round 1, structured screen", `evidence_defence` "Round 2, evidence defence", `panel` "Round 3, panel" (labels in `packages/i18n`, `interviews.round.*`), a check constraint matching `ROUNDS`.
- **Statuses:** `to_schedule` (no time yet, "Not scheduled yet": the pilot does not schedule, owner decision 24 Nov 2026), `scheduled`, `in_progress`, `scorecard_due`, `completed`, `cancelled`. Moves allowed by `canMove` only; `completed` and `cancelled` are final. A check constraint keeps a time on every `scheduled` interview and none on a `to_schedule` one.
- **Scorecard due:** computed, never written by a timer. `effectiveStatus` shows a `scheduled` interview whose time has passed as `scorecard_due`; one a person started stays `in_progress` until its scorecard is submitted (AIV-P2-006). `scorecardDueDays(scheduledAt, now, timeZone)` counts whole calendar days in the viewer's time zone for "Scorecard due, 2 days" (`interviews.scorecardDueFor`).
- **Contract:** `createInterview(ctx, { applicationId, round, interviewerUserId, scheduledAt?, competencyIds })` validates its input with zod. The job and candidate come from the stored application, never the request. It needs an approved rubric (`rubric_not_approved`), takes competencies from that version only ("This competency is not in the job's approved rubric"), and needs an interviewer who is an active member able to see the job (`interviewer_not_on_job`). Admins and recruiters on the job (`interview.schedule`); any refusal writes nothing. `startInterview(ctx, id)` moves to `in_progress`, only from the status it read (two concurrent starts cannot both apply); admins and recruiters, or the hiring owner or interviewer who holds the interview (`interview.conduct`). `listInterviews(ctx, 'ongoing' | 'completed')` and `getInterview(ctx, id)` return the status to show now, for jobs the context can see (`interview.view`); an interviewer sees only their own.
- **Events:** `InterviewScheduled.v1` (`{ interviewId, applicationId }`, strict, opaque ids only) is written to `interviews.outbox` in the same transaction when an interview gets a time. `InterviewCompleted.v1` has the same shape and is written by the scorecard submit (AIV-P2-006). Nothing consumes either yet: the worker relays this outbox for `MeetingSummarySubmitted.v1` (below) and parks these two until their first consumer maps them.
- **Row-level security (ADR-005):** every table `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with `tenantPolicy` on `workspace_id`; the repository also filters by workspace.
- **Scorecard drafts (AIV-P2-004):** `interviews.scorecard_drafts`, one row per interview (a foreign key to its interview, deleted with it): `competencies` (jsonb, per competency a rating, a note and the questions shown with their marks), `recommendation` (a check constraint matching `RECOMMENDATIONS`), who saved it and when. The shape is `scorecardDraftSchema` (`domain/scorecard.ts`), strict: ratings exist only per rubric competency, with no overall score, summary of the person, communication, English, confidence, attitude or soft-skill field, in the schema or the table (a snapshot test and a column test keep it so). `RATINGS` must list what `@credalent/assessment` applies (AIV-P1-117); a test in `apps/web` compares the two.
  - **Rules (pure):** `notAssessed(competency)`: every question marked `not_asked` or `not_answered` ("Not assessed in this round · stays a gap"). `assessedRatings(draft)`: the ratings the scorecard carries, none for a competency not assessed, whatever rating it held before, so it is never scored down. The score preview (AIV-P2-005) and the submit (AIV-P2-006) must read ratings only from it. `unrated(draft)` and `unexplained(draft)` (a rating with no note): what blocks a submit. `rebuildDraft(competencies, stored)`: the draft on the questions shown now, with the marks stored for the same words; the page starts from it and the submit rebuilds the stored draft with it. `toInterviewNoteEvidence({ interviewId, authorMemberId, draft })`: one `interview_note` payload per rated competency (competency, author, rating, note, interview) for the evidence contract's `recordEvidence`, which the submit writes. A question entry is `{ text, mark }`, so a guide's live task (AIV-P2-003) can count as one more entry.
  - **Contract:** `getScorecard(ctx, interviewId)` returns the interview (as `getInterview`), `canEdit` and the draft. `saveScorecardDraft(ctx, interviewId, draft)` replaces the draft and changes no score, stage or status. Only the interview's interviewer, on a job they can reach, or a workspace admin reads or saves a draft (`ForbiddenError` on save for a recruiter who is not the interviewer, and for an interviewer or hiring owner with no grant on the job, `scorecard.submit`); other members who see the interview get `canEdit: false` and no draft. A completed or cancelled interview takes no change (`scorecard_closed`); a competency the interview does not test is refused (`competency_not_in_interview`). A draft writes no audit entry and no event: nothing has been decided or scored yet.
  - **Score preview (AIV-P2-005):** `previewScorecardRatings(ctx, interviewId, ratings)` asks the assessment's `previewScoreChange` what the ratings (one per competency, `assessedRatings` of the draft) would do to the candidate's latest snapshot, through the same helper the meeting summary's preview uses, and writes nothing. Who as `saveScorecardDraft` (`scorecardHolder`): a recruiter or hiring owner who is not the interviewer gets `ForbiddenError`. `not_assessed` with no assessment yet, `preview_changed` when the assessment refuses it. The scorecard page shows it as "Effect on the assessment".
  - **Submit (AIV-P2-006):** `submitScorecard(ctx, interviewId, { previewId })` submits the stored draft in the caller's one transaction: the interview moves to `completed` only from the status it read (from `to_schedule`, `in_progress` or `scorecard_due`), so a second submit or a double click is `scorecard_submitted` ("Already submitted"); the draft, rebuilt on the questions the page shows (the assessment's stored suggested questions), is kept as the submitted scorecard with its recommendation, which changes no stage; `InterviewCompleted.v1` is written; each rated competency's note becomes `interview_note` evidence through the evidence contract's `recordInterviewSummary` (`from: 'scorecard'`, "Interviewer's scorecard, 26 Nov": Medium at the level the job needs for Clearly or Strongly shown, Claim only at no level for Not shown; Partly shown, which the preview shows changing nothing, records none and its note is the logged reason); and the assessment's `applyScoreChange` applies the ratings the person previewed (`previewId`, each note its reason, the interview id its idempotency key), its new snapshot and `score.changed` entries the last statements. A competency not assessed in this round has no rating, so no evidence, change or entry: it stays a gap. Who as `saveScorecardDraft`. Apps wire it with the same `decisions` as the meeting summary plus `suggestedQuestions`, the questions the page shows; both end in `recordAndApply` (`application/decision.ts`). A note is at most 1,000 characters (`NOTE_MAX`), the most the assessment logs as a reason. Refusals (`scorecard_incomplete`, `scorecard_closed`, `interview_not_held`, `not_assessed`, `competency_not_in_rubric`, `preview_changed`) write nothing.
    - shortcut: the live task's answer, time taken and prompt trail (AIV-P2-055, AIV-P2-056) are not in the draft yet, so the submit records none; those tickets add them to the draft and to the evidence the submit writes.
  - shortcut: a candidate's erasure does not reach interviews or their drafts yet (a note is the interviewer's words about a person); add it with the erasure ticket.
- **Meeting summaries (AIV-P1-115):** a pasted summary of an interview becomes proposed evidence changes a person confirms. Apps wire it with `createInterviews(..., now, decisions)`, where `decisions` are the assessment's `getLatestAssessment`, `previewScoreChange` and `applyScoreChange` and the evidence contract's `recordInterviewSummary`, as functions (and `suggestedQuestions` for the scorecard submit); the worker registers `createSummaryHandlers` on `SUMMARY_QUEUE`.
  - **Tables:** `interviews.meeting_summaries` (interview, author's user id, the text, status `analysing | waiting | failed | proposed | applied | declined`, `unusual`, the prompt version, created at; deleted with its interview), `interviews.summary_proposals` (one per summary and competency: finding, the excerpt and its span in the text, the model's reason, proposed strength `claim | medium`, status `proposed | applied | declined`; check constraints keep the strength below Strong and an excerpt on every finding but `not_mentioned`) and `interviews.inbox`. RLS with FORCE on all three.
  - **Commands:** `submitMeetingSummary(ctx, interviewId, text)` (1 to 12,000 characters, `summaryTextSchema`) stores the text and writes `MeetingSummarySubmitted.v1` (`{ summaryId, interviewId }`, strict, opaque ids only); it refuses a second summary while one is being analysed or waits for a decision. A summary still `analysing` 15 minutes after it was pasted (`ANALYSIS_BOUND_MS`: its analysis was dead-lettered) reads `failed` (`shownStatus`), so the page stops asking, and a new summary may be pasted, which marks the stalled one `failed` (`shortcut:` until the consumer can mark it on its last delivery). `getMeetingSummary(ctx, interviewId)` returns the newest summary's status and proposals, never its text, and while `proposed` the assessment's own preview: each change's strength before and after and the backed score for the changes included by default. `previewSummaryProposals(ctx, summaryId, competencyIds)` previews another choice. Every preview and the apply send their changes by competency id, so the preview id matches whatever order the panel sends them in. `applySummaryProposals(ctx, summaryId, { previewId, changes: [{ competencyId, reason }] })` and `declineSummaryProposals(ctx, summaryId)` decide once.
  - **The analysis** (`analyseSummary`, the worker's job `interviews.summary.analyse`): one call of `interviews.mapSummary` in `@credalent/ai` for the competencies the interview tests, labelled `C1`, `C2`, … so no opaque id reaches the model. The summary is the one source: wrapped as untrusted, with the candidate's name replaced by `[CANDIDATE]`, the interviewer's and the author's by `[PERSON]`, and every email and phone removed. The client rejects an excerpt that is not the summary's own words, a competency that was not listed, or a finding with no excerpt, asks once more, then the summary is `failed` ("The summary could not be analysed. You can rate on the scorecard instead."), as it is when the model stays unavailable. A spent daily AI budget makes it `waiting` and schedules the same message for 00:05 UTC the next day. A summary already analysed or decided is left alone, so a duplicated message stores one proposal set. It writes proposals only: no score, evidence or audit entry.
  - **Rules after the model (pure, `domain/summary.ts`):** Not shown proposes Claim only; Partly shown, no change; Clearly shown and Strongly shown, Medium: a summary is the interviewer's word, not inspected work, so nothing it says proposes Strong. A competency the answer leaves out is `not_mentioned` ("not covered in the summary · stays a gap"), and so is a finding whose excerpt lies in text aimed at an AI (`detectUnusualContent`), which also sets "Unusual content in this summary". The prompt's schema refuses a finding that rests on a phrase about the person ("his family", "seemed nervous", "her accent", nationality, religion), never on a work word alone ("health-check endpoint", "model family").
  - **Applying** runs in the caller's transaction, in this order: the summary and its proposals are marked (those not confirmed, declined); the evidence contract records the summary as an `interview_note` source "Interviewer's summary, 24 Nov" with one item per change citing its excerpt (Medium and `submitted` provenance at the level the job needs, shown as "Interviewer's word" on the profile and scorecard, never "Candidate-submitted", or Claim only for a weakening; never Strong, never corroborated); then the assessment's `applyScoreChange` stores one snapshot and audits each change with the person's reason for it, its audit entries being the last statements (AGENTS.md 3.6). A preview that is no longer the assessment's is `preview_changed` and nothing is stored. The summary's text reaches no log, event, error or audit value: only the reason the person confirmed is audited.
  - **"Already changed by the scorecard":** the assessment's preview says which rating of the same interview a change replaces; a competency so rated, and not by an applied summary of this interview, is flagged and excluded by default (`changedByScorecard`). The scorecard submit (AIV-P2-006) writes through the same command, so it needs nothing more here.
  - **A downgrade is off until a person includes it (AIV-P1-138):** a proposal that lowers the evidence (Not shown to Claim only, `lowersEvidence`) is excluded by default too, with the note "Lowers the evidence. Include it only if the summary says this was not shown.": the model may read silence as Not shown, and silence is a gap, never a reason to lower (AGENTS.md 1.6). `includedByDefault` is the one rule the read's preview and the panel's ticks use; raising proposals stay included.
  - **Who:** the people on the interview only: its interviewer, or an admin or recruiter, each on a job they can reach (`scorecard.submit`). Another interviewer, or a hiring owner who is not its interviewer, gets `ForbiddenError` and nothing is stored; they read no summary (`getMeetingSummary` is null). Another workspace's interview is `interview_not_found`.
  - shortcut: the analysis shares the `assessment` queue so the pilot needs no new queue; the erasure shortcut above covers summaries too (the text is the interviewer's words about a person).
- **Not here:** the Interviews page (AIV-P2-002) and the scorecard page (AIV-P2-004) are in `apps/web`; the guide and its live task (AIV-P2-003), the score preview's panel (AIV-P2-005), reading a submit's score changes back as an audit trail and the profile's "Interview history and score changes" (AIV-P1-046), scheduling and invitations (AIV-P2-007).
- **Demo seed:** `pnpm db:seed` adds the Interviews artboard's five ongoing interviews (`INTERVIEWS` in `tools/seed/src/cast.ts`), each on its candidate's application, on days counted from the day the seed runs. `purgeSyntheticInterviews(ctx)` is the seed's reset: admins, and only in a workspace flagged synthetic (`not_synthetic` otherwise, deleting nothing). The reset cannot delete outbox rows, so each one leaves five unsent `InterviewScheduled.v1` events for interviews that are gone: the consumer that first relays this outbox (AIV-P2-006) must accept an interview that no longer exists.

Meeting summaries: `src/domain/summary.test.ts`, `src/application/summary.test.ts`, `tests/integration/src/meeting-summary.test.ts`, the eval set `packages/ai/evals/interviews.mapSummary/` and the J3 step `tests/e2e/src/meeting-summary.spec.ts`.
