# @credalent/interviews

Interviews exist to test specific evidence (AIV-P2-001). Each one links a candidate's application on one job (`@credalent/intake`) to a round, an interviewer and the competencies of the job's approved rubric version (`@credalent/jobs`) it must investigate. Other code imports only `@credalent/interviews` (the `InterviewsContract` types); apps build it with `createInterviews(workspaces, jobs, approvedRubric, findApplication, now)` from `@credalent/interviews/composition`, where `approvedRubric` is the jobs composition's `createApprovedRubricReads().approvedRubric` and `findApplication` is the intake composition's. Both are unauthorised reads, called only for the job the use case then authorises; they are passed as functions so intake can call this contract (AIV-P1-114) without a package cycle.

- **Tables:** `interviews.interview` (application, job and candidate ids, round, interviewer user id, `scheduled_at`, status, the rubric version and its `competency_ids`, who created it and when) `interviews.scorecard_drafts` (below) and `interviews.outbox`. Other modules' ids are opaque: no foreign keys across modules. Migrations are tracked in `migrations.interviews`.
- **Rounds:** `structured_screen` "Round 1, structured screen", `evidence_defence` "Round 2, evidence defence", `panel` "Round 3, panel" (labels in `packages/i18n`, `interviews.round.*`), a check constraint matching `ROUNDS`.
- **Statuses:** `to_schedule` (no time yet, "Not scheduled yet": the pilot does not schedule, owner decision 24 Nov 2026), `scheduled`, `in_progress`, `scorecard_due`, `completed`, `cancelled`. Moves allowed by `canMove` only; `completed` and `cancelled` are final. A check constraint keeps a time on every `scheduled` interview and none on a `to_schedule` one.
- **Scorecard due:** computed, never written by a timer. `effectiveStatus` shows a `scheduled` interview whose time has passed as `scorecard_due`; one a person started stays `in_progress` until its scorecard is submitted (AIV-P2-006). `scorecardDueDays(scheduledAt, now, timeZone)` counts whole calendar days in the viewer's time zone for "Scorecard due, 2 days" (`interviews.scorecardDueFor`).
- **Contract:** `createInterview(ctx, { applicationId, round, interviewerUserId, scheduledAt?, competencyIds })` validates its input with zod. The job and candidate come from the stored application, never the request. It needs an approved rubric (`rubric_not_approved`), takes competencies from that version only ("This competency is not in the job's approved rubric"), and needs an interviewer who is an active member able to see the job (`interviewer_not_on_job`). Admins and recruiters on the job (`interview.schedule`); any refusal writes nothing. `startInterview(ctx, id)` moves to `in_progress`, only from the status it read (two concurrent starts cannot both apply); admins and recruiters, or the hiring owner or interviewer who holds the interview (`interview.conduct`). `listInterviews(ctx, 'ongoing' | 'completed')` and `getInterview(ctx, id)` return the status to show now, for jobs the context can see (`interview.view`); an interviewer sees only their own.
- **Events:** `InterviewScheduled.v1` (`{ interviewId, applicationId }`, strict, opaque ids only) is written to `interviews.outbox` in the same transaction when an interview gets a time. `InterviewCompleted.v1` has the same shape and is written by the scorecard submit (AIV-P2-006). Nothing consumes either yet, so the worker does not relay this outbox; they wait unsent until score changes from scorecards (AIV-P2-005) map them.
- **Row-level security (ADR-005):** every table `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with `tenantPolicy` on `workspace_id`; the repository also filters by workspace.
- **Scorecard drafts (AIV-P2-004):** `interviews.scorecard_drafts`, one row per interview (a foreign key to its interview, deleted with it): `competencies` (jsonb, per competency a rating, a note and the questions shown with their marks), `recommendation` (a check constraint matching `RECOMMENDATIONS`), who saved it and when. The shape is `scorecardDraftSchema` (`domain/scorecard.ts`), strict: ratings exist only per rubric competency, with no overall score, summary of the person, communication, English, confidence, attitude or soft-skill field, in the schema or the table (a snapshot test and a column test keep it so). `RATINGS` must list what `@credalent/assessment` applies (AIV-P1-117); a test in `apps/web` compares the two.
  - **Rules (pure):** `notAssessed(competency)`: every question marked `not_asked` or `not_answered` ("Not assessed in this round · stays a gap"). `assessedRatings(draft)`: the ratings the scorecard carries, none for a competency not assessed, whatever rating it held before, so it is never scored down. The score preview (AIV-P2-005) and the submit (AIV-P2-006) must read ratings only from it. `unrated(draft)`: what blocks a submit. `toInterviewNoteEvidence({ interviewId, authorMemberId, draft })`: one `interview_note` payload per rated competency (competency, author, rating, note, interview) for the evidence contract's `recordEvidence`, which the submit writes. A question entry is `{ text, mark }`, so a guide's live task (AIV-P2-003) can count as one more entry.
  - **Contract:** `getScorecard(ctx, interviewId)` returns the interview (as `getInterview`), `canEdit` and the draft. `saveScorecardDraft(ctx, interviewId, draft)` replaces the draft and changes no score, stage or status. Only the interview's interviewer, on a job they can reach, or a workspace admin reads or saves a draft (`ForbiddenError` on save for a recruiter who is not the interviewer, and for an interviewer or hiring owner with no grant on the job, `scorecard.submit`); other members who see the interview get `canEdit: false` and no draft. A completed or cancelled interview takes no change (`scorecard_closed`); a competency the interview does not test is refused (`competency_not_in_interview`). A draft writes no audit entry and no event: nothing has been decided or scored yet.
  - shortcut: a candidate's erasure does not reach interviews or their drafts yet (a note is the interviewer's words about a person); add it with the submit (AIV-P2-006) or the erasure ticket, whichever lands first.
- **Not here:** the Interviews page (AIV-P2-002) and the scorecard page (AIV-P2-004) are in `apps/web`; the guide and its live task (AIV-P2-003), the score preview (AIV-P2-005), submitting a scorecard, its score change, evidence and audit trail (AIV-P2-006), scheduling and invitations (AIV-P2-007).
- **Demo seed:** `pnpm db:seed` adds the Interviews artboard's five ongoing interviews (`INTERVIEWS` in `tools/seed/src/cast.ts`), each on its candidate's application, on days counted from the day the seed runs. `purgeSyntheticInterviews(ctx)` is the seed's reset: admins, and only in a workspace flagged synthetic (`not_synthetic` otherwise, deleting nothing). The reset cannot delete outbox rows, so each one leaves five unsent `InterviewScheduled.v1` events for interviews that are gone: the consumer that first relays this outbox (AIV-P2-005) must accept an interview that no longer exists.
- **Tests:** transitions, labels and scorecard due in `src/domain/interview.test.ts`; use-case rules in `src/application/interviews.test.ts`; database scenarios in `tests/integration/src/interviews.test.ts`.
