# @aivera/jobs

Jobs and their own versioned rubrics (AIV-P1-005), and the job types catalogue (AIV-P1-004). Every job has its own rubric; editing an approved rubric starts a new version that needs approval, and the approved version stays in force until then. Assessment reads only the approved version. Other code imports only `@aivera/jobs` (the `JobsContract` types); apps build it with `createJobs(workspaces, audit, now)` from `@aivera/jobs/composition`.

- **Job types:** `JOB_TYPE_CATALOGUE` in `src/domain/job-types.ts` lists the ten job types in PRODUCT.md's order. Each has a `key`, a `labelKey` and an `evidenceKey` (`jobTypes.<key>.*` in `packages/i18n`), and four starter competencies. It is code, reviewed in PRs, with no table. Bump `JOB_TYPE_CATALOGUE_VERSION` when an entry changes meaning. Keys are stored as a workspace's hiring focus and a job's type, so a key is never renamed.
- **Tables (schema `jobs`, tracked in `migrations.jobs`):** `job` (title, job type, description, team, client id, location, hiring owner user id, stage, published, public descriptor (how the candidate site names the employer, never the client's name), published at, pay band in integer minor units plus currency, `synthetic`, created by and at); `rubric_version` (job, version number, status `draft | awaiting_approval | approved | superseded | returned`, success statement, change note, created by and at, approved by and at, who last sent it for approval and the hiring owner's return note (AIV-P1-014)); `competency` (version, name, evidence expected, weight percent, level needed, type `mandatory | desirable`, origin `from_description | suggested_by_aivera | added_by_recruiter`, position). The client and hiring owner are opaque ids of the workspaces module (no foreign key across modules); child rows are keyed with the workspace, so they cannot point into another workspace.
- **Rules, in the domain and in the database (AGENTS.md 3.5):** weights in a version total 100 (`assertValidCompetencies`, and a deferred constraint trigger `competency_weights_total_check` at commit); each weight 1–100 and level 1–5 (check constraints); at most one approved version per job (partial unique index `rubric_version_one_approved_idx`) and at most one open one, draft, awaiting approval or returned (`rubric_version_one_open_idx`, AIV-P1-013); approving supersedes the previous approved version in the same transaction, under a lock on the job row; an approved or superseded version, its revision and its competencies never change (triggers `rubric_version_frozen_check`, `competency_version_frozen_check`).
- **Contract:** `getJob`, `getJobHeader` (AIV-P1-011: the job, its approved and latest rubric versions, and the assessed count), `listJobs`, `listJobsForDesk` (the Jobs list rows: rubric state and next action), `getApprovedRubric(jobId)` (null when none is approved), `listRubricVersions(jobId)` (AIV-P1-012: every version with its competencies, newest first, for the rubric page and its approval history), and `createJob`, `createJobWithDraftRubric` (AIV-P1-010: the job with rubric version 1 as a draft in one transaction, weights rebalanced to 100, audited as `job.created`), `saveRubric` (AIV-P1-013: one open version per job, which editing resumes; a save from an older copy, by `base` version and revision, throws `JobError` `stale`; without a note, the change note is written from the changes to the approved version), `submitRubric` (audited as `rubric.submitted`, recording who sent it; the approved version stays in force), `approveRubric` and `returnRubric(note)` (AIV-P1-014: only the job's own hiring owner, or an admin acting as owner and audited as `actingAs: 'admin_as_owner'`; a decision on an older revision throws `JobError` `stale`; approval writes `RubricApproved.v1` to the outbox in the same transaction), `listRubricApprovals` (My desk: versions awaiting approval and those approved in the past week), and for the candidate site `getPublicListing(jobId)` (the allow-listed listing for the publish preview: title, job type, descriptor, location, pay band, competencies with evidence expected and level; no weight, client, description or ids), `publishJob(jobId, { publicDescriptor })` (needs an approved rubric) and `unpublishJob(jobId)`, both audited, and `purgeSyntheticJobs` (AIV-P1-065: the demo seed's reset; admins; refused unless the workspace and every job in it is synthetic, and it cannot delete an approved rubric version). Each takes the request context from `WorkspacesContract.withWorkspace` and runs in its scoped transaction. Rule failures throw `JobError`; refusals `ForbiddenError`.
- **Authorisation:** through the workspaces `authorize`: `job.create` and `rubric.edit` for admins and recruiters, `rubric.approve` for admins and granted hiring owners, narrowed in the module to the job's own hiring owner (`approvesAs`), `job.view` (job-scoped) and `job.list` for every role.
- **Row-level security (ADR-005):** all three tables `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with `tenantPolicy` on `workspace_id`. Job scope (point 6): `job` has the restrictive policy `job_scope` (`app.job_scope = 'all'`, or the job id is in `app.job_ids`, or its client id in `app.client_ids`), and `rubric_version` and `competency` have restrictive policies that require their job (through the version) to be visible, so a hiring owner or interviewer cannot read another job's rubric either. The repository filters by the workspace and by the same job-scope expression (`jobInScope`).
- **Audit:** `job.created`, `rubric.saved` (with the status it had, so taking a version back from awaiting approval shows), `rubric.submitted`, `rubric.approved` and `rubric.returned` (actor, time, before and after, the return note and whether an admin acted as owner) are written last in the transaction (AGENTS.md 3.6).
- **Public read model (AIV-P3-002):** `public_job`, one row per job ever published, written in the publishing transaction by `publishJob`, `unpublishJob` (sets `open` false, so an old link can say "no longer open") and `approveRubric` (a published job follows its approved version; a draft never reaches it). It holds the allow-listed listing, a slug (title plus a random tail, kept across republishing) and the posted date. `createPublicJobs(db)` gives `PublicJobsContract` (`listPublicJobs`, `findPublicJob(slug)`), which reads with no workspace as the `aivera_public` role: it may select the public columns of `public_job` (`grants.sql`) and nothing else, through the one non-tenant policy `public_job_public_read`. Public responses go out through the strict zod schema `publicJobJson`. `paused` (AIV-P2-037) hides every listing of a suspended workspace: `listPublicJobs` leaves it out and `findPublicJob` reads it as no longer open, with `open` kept so reactivation restores exactly what was listed. Only the operator's status change sets it, through `pausePublicListings(tx, workspaceId, paused)` inside that change's `withWorkspace(<workspace>)` transaction (architecture rule `operator-only-writes`).
- **Events:** `RubricApproved.v1` (opaque ids only) is written to `jobs.outbox` by `approveRubric` in the approval's transaction (AIV-P1-014) and relayed to the `assessment` queue; it is the only thing that starts assessment (AIV-P1-031 consumes it). `JobPublished.v1` and `JobUnpublished.v1` are defined in the contract and written by `publishJob` and `unpublishJob` with AIV-P1-015.
- **Not here:** the approval emails, queued by the web app through `@aivera/notifications` in the same transaction (AIV-P1-014), screens (AIV-P1-006, AIV-P1-007, AIV-P1-012) including the rubric-page Publish panel (AIV-P1-015), the candidate pages (AIV-P3-003 to P3-005), the demo seed's five design jobs (they live in `tools/seed/src/cast.ts` and the e2e seed until their stages can be reached through the contract; `pnpm db:seed` writes the five industry jobs of AIV-P1-065).
- **Tests:** rules in `src/domain/job.test.ts`; the catalogue in `src/domain/job-types.test.ts`; database scenarios in `tests/integration/src/jobs.test.ts`.
