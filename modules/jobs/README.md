# @credalent/jobs

Jobs and their own versioned rubrics (AIV-P1-005), and the job types catalogue (AIV-P1-004). Every job has its own rubric; editing an approved rubric starts a new version that needs approval, and the approved version stays in force until then. Assessment reads only the approved version. Other code imports only `@credalent/jobs` (the `JobsContract` types); apps build it with `createJobs(workspaces, audit, now)` from `@credalent/jobs/composition`.

- **Job types and domain packs** live in the catalogue tables (below, [ADR-013](../../docs/adr/ADR-013-catalogue-in-the-database.md)). Version 1 holds the ten job types in PRODUCT.md's order, each with its label and "Evidence that counts" per locale and four starter competencies, and the eight domain packs with their competencies and regional hints. Keys are stored as a workspace's hiring focus, a job's type and a kept competency's pack, so a key is never renamed; an entry is retired, never deleted. Pure helpers over a catalogue value are in `src/domain/catalogue.ts`: `offeredJobTypes`, `jobTypeLabel`, `packForJobType` (a pack as one job type sees it, retired entries left out), `isRegionalHint` and `nameKey`.
- **Tables (schema `jobs`, tracked in `migrations.jobs`):** `job` (title, job type, description, team, client id, location, hiring owner user id, stage, published, public descriptor (how the candidate site names the employer, never the client's name), published at, pay band in integer minor units plus currency, `synthetic`, created by and at); `rubric_version` (job, version number, status `draft | awaiting_approval | approved | superseded | returned`, success statement, change note, created by and at, approved by and at, who last sent it for approval and the hiring owner's return note (AIV-P1-014)); `competency` (version, name, evidence expected, weight percent, level needed, type `mandatory | desirable`, origin `from_description | suggested_by_aivera | added_by_recruiter`, position). The client and hiring owner are opaque ids of the workspaces module (no foreign key across modules); child rows are keyed with the workspace, so they cannot point into another workspace.
- **Rules, in the domain and in the database (AGENTS.md 3.5):** weights in a version total 100 (`assertValidCompetencies`, and a deferred constraint trigger `competency_weights_total_check` at commit); each weight 1–100 and level 1–5 (check constraints); at most one approved version per job (partial unique index `rubric_version_one_approved_idx`) and at most one open one, draft, awaiting approval or returned (`rubric_version_one_open_idx`, AIV-P1-013); approving supersedes the previous approved version in the same transaction, under a lock on the job row; an approved or superseded version, its revision and its competencies never change (triggers `rubric_version_frozen_check`, `competency_version_frozen_check`).
- **Contract:** `getJob`, `getJobHeader` (AIV-P1-011: the job, its approved and latest rubric versions, the assessed count, and while published its candidate-site slug, AIV-P1-015), `listJobs`, `listJobsForDesk` (the Jobs list rows: rubric state and next action), `getApprovedRubric(jobId)` (null when none is approved), `listRubricVersions(jobId)` (AIV-P1-012: every version with its competencies, newest first, for the rubric page and its approval history), and `createJob`, `createJobWithDraftRubric` (AIV-P1-010: the job with rubric version 1 as a draft in one transaction, weights rebalanced to 100, audited as `job.created`), `saveRubric` (AIV-P1-013: one open version per job, which editing resumes; a save from an older copy, by `base` version and revision, throws `JobError` `stale`; without a note, the change note is written from the changes to the approved version), `submitRubric` (audited as `rubric.submitted`, recording who sent it; the approved version stays in force), `approveRubric` and `returnRubric(note)` (AIV-P1-014: an admin or recruiter on the job (`actingAs: 'recruiter'`), a draft directly, or the job's own hiring owner for a version sent to them (`actingAs: 'owner'`), who alone returns one (ADR-014); a decision on an older revision throws `JobError` `stale`; approval writes `RubricApproved.v1` to the outbox in the same transaction), `listRubricApprovals` (My desk's "Waiting on others": versions awaiting approval and those the hiring owner approved in the past week; one a recruiter approved is left out, ADR-014), `listRubricsToApprove` (My desk's "Rubrics to approve", AIV-P1-084: each job's open draft or returned version, for admins and recruiters; empty for other roles), and for the candidate site `getPublicListing(jobId)` (the allow-listed listing for the publish preview: title, job type, descriptor, location, pay band, competencies with evidence expected and level; no weight, client, description or ids), `publishJob(jobId, { publicDescriptor })` (needs an approved rubric) and `unpublishJob(jobId)`, both audited, and `purgeSyntheticJobs` (AIV-P1-065: the demo seed's reset; admins; refused unless the workspace and every job in it is synthetic, and it cannot delete an approved rubric version). Each takes the request context from `WorkspacesContract.withWorkspace` and runs in its scoped transaction. Rule failures throw `JobError`; refusals `ForbiddenError`.
- **Authorisation:** through the workspaces `authorize`: `job.create` and `rubric.edit` for admins and recruiters, `rubric.approve` for admins, recruiters and granted hiring owners, narrowed in the module to admins, recruiters and the job's own hiring owner (`approvesAs`); a hiring owner decides only a version sent to them ([ADR-014](../../docs/adr/ADR-014-recruiters-approve-rubrics.md)), `job.view` (job-scoped) and `job.list` for every role.
- **Row-level security (ADR-005):** all three tables `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with `tenantPolicy` on `workspace_id`. Job scope (point 6): `job` has the restrictive policy `job_scope` (`app.job_scope = 'all'`, or the job id is in `app.job_ids`, or its client id in `app.client_ids`), and `rubric_version` and `competency` have restrictive policies that require their job (through the version) to be visible, so a hiring owner or interviewer cannot read another job's rubric either. The repository filters by the workspace and by the same job-scope expression (`jobInScope`).
- **Audit:** `job.created`, `rubric.saved` (with the status it had, so taking a version back from awaiting approval shows), `rubric.submitted`, `rubric.approved` and `rubric.returned` (actor, time, before and after, the return note and `actingAs`: `recruiter` or `owner`; older entries may say `admin_as_owner`) are written last in the transaction (AGENTS.md 3.6).
- **Public read model (AIV-P3-002):** `public_job`, one row per job ever published, written in the publishing transaction by `publishJob`, `unpublishJob` (sets `open` false, so an old link can say "no longer open") and `approveRubric` (a published job follows its approved version; a draft never reaches it). It holds the allow-listed listing, a slug (title plus a random tail, kept across republishing) and the posted date. `createPublicJobs(db)` gives `PublicJobsContract` (`listPublicJobs`, `findPublicJob(slug)`), which reads with no workspace as the `aivera_public` role: it may select the public columns of `public_job` (`grants.sql`) and nothing else, through the one non-tenant policy `public_job_public_read`. Public responses go out through the strict zod schema `publicJobJson`. `paused` (AIV-P2-037) hides every listing of a suspended workspace: `listPublicJobs` leaves it out and `findPublicJob` reads it as no longer open, with `open` kept so reactivation restores exactly what was listed. Only the operator's status change sets it, through `pausePublicListings(tx, workspaceId, paused)` inside that change's `withWorkspace(<workspace>)` transaction (architecture rule `operator-only-writes`).
- **Events:** `RubricApproved.v1` (opaque ids only) is written to `jobs.outbox` by `approveRubric` in the approval's transaction (AIV-P1-014) and relayed to the `assessment` queue; it is the only thing that starts assessment (AIV-P1-031 consumes it). `JobPublished.v1` and `JobUnpublished.v1` (AIV-P1-015) are written by `publishJob` and `unpublishJob` in their transactions and relayed to the `candidates` queue (nothing consumes them until AIV-P3-008). `CatalogueEvalRequested.v1` (AIV-P2-048) goes to the `jobs` queue, which this module consumes.
- **Not here:** the approval emails, queued by the web app through `@credalent/notifications` in the same transaction (AIV-P1-014), screens (AIV-P1-006, AIV-P1-007, AIV-P1-012), the candidate pages (AIV-P3-003 to P3-005), the demo seed's five design jobs (they live in `tools/seed/src/cast.ts` and the e2e seed until their stages can be reached through the contract; `pnpm db:seed` writes the five industry jobs of AIV-P1-065).
- **Catalogue in the database (AIV-P2-043, [ADR-013](../../docs/adr/ADR-013-catalogue-in-the-database.md)):** platform tables with no `workspace_id` and no tenant policy: `catalogue_version` (version number, status `draft | published`, created by and at, published by and at, reason; actors are an operator's opaque user id or `system:seed`; at most one draft, `catalogue_version_one_draft_idx`) and, per version, `catalogue_job_type` (key, position, label and evidence per locale as `{ en, ar? }`, four starters, retired), `catalogue_region`, `catalogue_pack`, `catalogue_pack_competency` (name, meaning, evidence, levels 1/3/5, applies-to and core job types, level needed 2 or 3, type `desirable`, retired) and `catalogue_regional_hint` (the same per region, without core). Keys follow `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{2,47}$` and are never `none`, `several` or `other`; the same format check replaced the key lists on `job.job_type` and `competency.pack_key`. A published version never changes: triggers (`catalogue_version_frozen_check`, migration 0011) refuse `UPDATE`, `DELETE` and `TRUNCATE` for every role. Migration 0012 seeds published version 1 from `src/infrastructure/catalogue-v1.json`, the snapshot of the code catalogue taken before AIV-P2-044 deleted it; it is generated by `pnpm --filter @credalent/seed catalogue:v1` and reruns as a no-op. The AI eval runner and unit tests read that snapshot; the integration suite proves the database publishes exactly it.
- **Catalogue reads (AIV-P2-044, ADR-013 point 15):** `createCatalogueReads(db)` gives `CatalogueReads`: `getPublishedCatalogue()` (the latest published version, which Add a job, the marketing page, both sign-ups, the candidate pages, the seed and the AI calls read). `createJobWithDraftRubric` and `createJob` refuse a job type the published version does not offer (`JobError` `job_type`, a retired one included); `assertValidCompetencies` accepts a pack of the published version, retired too, at a version no newer than it, and keeps regional hints desirable. `draftCompetencies` and `detectDomain` take the published catalogue: the first refuses a job type it does not offer, the second puts its packs and regions not retired in the output schema, and both record its version on telemetry next to the prompt version (`+catalogue@N`).
- **Catalogue editor (AIV-P2-045, ADR-013 points 3 to 6, 11, 14):** `createCatalogueEditor(db, audit, now)` gives `CatalogueEditor`, for `/ops` only (behind `platformOperator()`; architecture rule `operator-only-writes`): `getEditor()` (the latest published version and the open draft, competencies with their `position`), `openDraft` (copies the latest published version into version N+1 as the one draft), `saveDraftPack` (add, or edit the English label; a key changes only while the pack is new in the draft, never to one the draft holds), `saveDraftCompetency` (add or edit, always `desirable`) and `retireDraftEntry` (`{ entry: "pack" | "competency", … }`: retires what a published version holds, removes what is new in the draft; a retired entry is never edited or retired again). Inputs are the zod schemas `draftPackSchema`, `draftCompetencySchema` and `retireEntrySchema`. Every write locks the draft row and names the draft `version` and the `revision` it read (`catalogue_version.revision` and `revised_by`, migration 0018): another draft or an older revision throws `CatalogueError('stale')` with who saved last. The fairness check (`fairnessRule`, `assertFairCompetency` in `src/domain/catalogue-draft.ts`: empty evidence, an employer, a nationality or where someone is from, whole words only; version 1 passes it) and at most `CORE_LIMIT` (4) core competencies per job type per pack (`assertCompetencyFits`) refuse a save with the rule and the form field, never the text. Entry status against the published version: `packStatus`, `competencyStatus` (live, changed, new, retired), `isNewInDraft`. Each write audits `catalogue.draft_opened`, `catalogue.entry_added`, `catalogue.entry_edited`, `catalogue.entry_retired` or `catalogue.entry_removed` in `audit.platform_entry` with the operator id and the draft's version, revision, pack key and position, last in its transaction. Job types (AIV-P2-047): `saveDraftJobType` (`draftJobTypeSchema`: add with `adding`, or edit the English label, evidence text and exactly four starters; `position` places it in the Add a job list, `placeJobType`; the key never changes and is refused as `key_taken` when any version holds it, retired keys included; starters pass `assertFairStarters`), `retireDraftEntry` with `{ entry: "jobType", jobTypeKey }` (a new job type a pack entry applies to is not removed, `job_type_in_use`) and `jobTypeStatus`. A new job type is in no pack until the competency form adds it. Audited with `jobTypeKey`.
- **Catalogue draft eval (AIV-P2-048, ADR-013 point 12):** the editor's `requestEval(operatorId, { version, revision })` ("Run the eval"; `evalRequestSchema`) starts a run on the draft as the page showed it (`stale` otherwise; `eval_running` while another run on the draft runs, `catalogue_eval_run_one_running_idx`), stores the draft's content hash (`contentHash`, SHA-256 of its entries with keys in a canonical order) and the pack and region keys not retired in `jobs.catalogue_eval_run` (migration 0019, a platform table), writes `CatalogueEvalRequested.v1` (the run id) to `jobs.outbox` and audits `catalogue.eval_requested` last. Its transaction runs in a fixed synthetic workspace scope, `CATALOGUE_EVAL_SCOPE`, because the outbox's tenant policy needs one. The worker consumes it from the `jobs` queue (`createCatalogueEvalHandler`, with `jobs.inbox`, migrations 0019 and 0020): the golden sets of `@credalent/ai/evals` with the run's keys, within `liveTokenCap` and the platform eval budget left today (`EVAL_DAILY_TOKEN_CAP`, summed from the runs' `tokens`), and records `passed`, `failed` (with per-task rates, failed case ids and pack keys with no golden case) or `error` (the model did not answer) once; a redelivery does nothing. `getDraftEval()` gives `DraftEval`: `passed` only when the latest run passed on the draft's current content hash, `stale` after any edit, `error` for a run still running after 30 minutes (which no longer blocks a new one). Publish (AIV-P2-049) and the console home (AIV-P2-051) read it; `evalAllowsPublish` is the gate.
- **Publish or discard the catalogue draft (AIV-P2-049, ADR-013 points 4, 13, 14):** `publishDraft(operatorId, { version, revision, reason })` (`publishDraftSchema`, reason 10 to 500 characters) locks the draft, refuses it unless the latest eval run passed on the locked draft's current content hash (`eval_required`, read in the transaction, never from the client) and the draft changes something (`no_changes`), then sets the draft row `published` with the operator, time and reason in one update: `readPublishedCatalogue` takes the newest published version, so every reader switches at commit and never sees half of it, and the freeze triggers of migration 0016 hold its rows from then on. Audited `catalogue.published` with the reason as note and `added`, `edited`, `retired` counts. `discardDraft(operatorId, { version, revision })` (`discardDraftSchema`) deletes the draft's rows (`eval_running` while a run is running); its eval runs stay with `version` null (migration 0021, `ON DELETE SET NULL`), so a discard never lowers the day's eval token spend, audited `catalogue.draft_discarded`. Both lock the draft row like every edit, so a racing edit, eval request, publish or discard makes the second one `stale` or `no_draft` with nothing changed. The next draft reuses a discarded draft's number: the platform log tells drafts apart by order, each running from its `catalogue.draft_opened` to its `catalogue.draft_discarded` or `catalogue.published`. `catalogueDiff(published, draft)` lists the preview's changes (added; edited with each field's before and after; retired; one entry for a reorder of published job types), grouped by job types, packs, competencies and regional hints.
- **Regional hints in the draft (AIV-P2-046, ADR-013 point 11):** `saveDraftHint` adds or edits a pack's hint for one region of the draft that is not retired (the editor's hint sections follow the draft's regions; adding a region is not built yet) (`draftHintSchema`: the competency fields without core, plus `region`; strict, so a `type` is refused; the database's `catalogue_regional_hint_type_check` allows only `desirable`). `assertFairHint` runs the residency rule (`residencyRule`: a residence or residency, a work or entry visa or permit, holding a visa, ID, iqama or Aadhaar, sponsorship or kafala, a passport, nationality, living or based in, "candidates from"; "data residency", "tax residency" and "Emirates ID" sign-in pass; version 1 passes it) before the competency rules and refuses with `fairness_residency`. A retired job type stays on a hint that already had it, as on a competency (AIV-P2-047). `retireDraftEntry` takes `{ entry: "regionalHint", packKey, region, position }`; a hint's position counts within its pack and region. `hintStatus` and `isHintNewInDraft` give its status. Audited like a competency, with `region` added to `PLATFORM_VALUE_KEYS`. Tested in `tests/integration/src/catalogue-hints.test.ts`.
