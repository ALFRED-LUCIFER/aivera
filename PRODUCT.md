# Aivera: evidence intelligence for hiring

**Hire on evidence, not claims.** Aivera reads the work candidates have already done, scores it against the competencies a hiring manager agreed, and shows what every score rests on. Recruiters interview fewer people and can defend each decision.

## Who it is for

| Who                                               | What they need from Aivera                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Recruiter** (in-house talent partner or agency) | One place from job description to decision: rubric, inbox, ranking, evidence requests, interviews, decision pack. Fewer interviews per hire. |
| **Hiring owner** (e.g. Head of Engineering)       | Approve the rubric; decide on a shortlist with the evidence in front of them.                                                                |
| **Interviewer**                                   | A scorecard with questions tied to the gaps in each candidate's evidence.                                                                    |
| **Client** (agency recruiting)                    | A shared shortlist with summaries and sources, to answer Interview, Hold or Pass.                                                            |
| **Candidate**                                     | Know what a job needs before applying; be judged on work, not keywords; never rejected by a machine.                                         |

The demo cast is synthetic: [`docs/00-product/personas.md`](docs/00-product/personas.md).

## Three surfaces

1. **Recruiter app**: My desk, Inbox, Jobs (add a job, rubric), Candidates (ranking, evidence profile, compare), Talent pool, Interviews (scorecard), Decision packs, Value dashboard, and the Aivera assistant on every page.
2. **External pages**: marketing website, sign up and sign in, the candidate evidence-upload page, the client view of a shortlist.
3. **Candidate site**: jobs with their competencies shown up front, apply, free CV check, my applications. **Plus** (AED 49 a month): portfolio at the candidate's own address, CV tailored per job, gap report, interview preparation, fit-ranked alerts.

## Principles (each is a rule in AGENTS.md §1)

1. Every score cites the work it rests on.
2. Claims are labelled as claims and count for nothing until something supports them.
3. Nobody is rejected automatically. Aivera recommends; a person decides.
4. Numbers say what they are: observed, baseline or projection.
5. Thin evidence is a gap to close, not a reason to reject.
6. Paying never changes how recruiters rank a candidate.

## How we are judged

One number: **interviews per completed hire.** A pilot on one role passes if it holds 30% fewer interviews per hire than the customer's own baseline.

## Role families covered at launch

Software engineering · QA and test automation · AI and LLM engineering · Forward deployed engineering · Data and machine learning · DevOps and SRE · Product management · Product design · Sales · Technical writing and support. Each family has its own kinds of evidence; candidates with no public work get a short, role-scoped work sample, labelled as one.
